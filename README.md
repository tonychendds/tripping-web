# Tripping (web)

A free, local-first trip itinerary. Open it at **https://tonychendds.github.io/tripping-web/**.

Everything you enter stays in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server, and each person who opens the link has their own separate data. Use **Export** to save a backup file, and **Import** to bring one back. **Add trip** works without an account.

The starter trips keep dates, the hotel, the city, and the flight route. Confirmation codes, loyalty numbers, ticket numbers, and card details were removed before this site was published. Add those on your device, or import a private export. The import stays in that browser.

This repository only holds the published website files.
